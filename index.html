The Space

Nestled at the edge of the forested hills of Arima, Greene Kairi is a peaceful retreat where nature and comfort come together. Wake up to misty mountain views, the melodies of tropical birds, and the gentle breeze rustling through the rainforest canopy.

Whether you're seeking a quiet escape, a romantic getaway, or a comfortable base to explore Trinidad, our cozy studio offers the perfect balance of relaxation and convenience. Sip your morning coffee surrounded by lush greenery, unwind in a tranquil setting, and experience the warmth of authentic Trinbago hospitality.

Just minutes from Arima's town center yet surrounded by nature, Greene Kairi is your home away from the ordinary—a place to slow down, recharge, and reconnect with what matters most.
